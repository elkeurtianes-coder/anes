# Anes — product demos

A small collection of static prototypes for business websites, content workflows, and campaign analysis.

## Projects

- **Creative Workbench** — turn a brand brief and manual ad observations into original short-form angles, a script draft, five video-generation prompts, and a caption.
- **Prospect Board** — a local lead tracker with CSV import/export and duplicate checks.
- **Campaign Dashboard** — explore a synthetic campaign dataset or upload a CSV for local analysis.
- **Cafe Demo** — a fictional cafe website concept with a sample menu.

## Run locally

Open each project's `index.html` in a modern browser:

- `commercial-studio/apps/creative-workbench/index.html`
- `commercial-studio/apps/prospect-board/index.html`
- `commercial-studio/apps/campaign-dashboard/index.html`
- `commercial-studio/sites/cafe-demo/index.html`

## Prototype notes

These demos are static front-end prototypes. They have no server, account system, AI API, or automatic posting. The Prospect Board and Creative Workbench store data in the current browser's local storage. Campaign examples are synthetic. Cafe brand, menu, prices, address, and hours are fictional placeholders.

Vercel deployments exist, but their current deployment-protection settings require review before public live-demo links can be shared.
